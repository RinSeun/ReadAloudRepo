Lotus Aloud: Privacy Policy

BY LOTUS SHIELD STUDIO

Last updated: November 2026

Lotus Aloud reads text out loud: text you highlight, share, paste or copy, webpages you share, and documents and pictures you choose. This page explains what happens to your information. In short: we don't collect anything, and we don't run any servers that receive your data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings, voices and API keys for voice services. They are stored only on your phone and never sent to us.
  • Recent reads and saved audio, so you can replay things. You can clear them at any time by clearing the app's storage.
  • Pictures and documents you choose. They are read on your phone to find the text, then the copies are deleted.

What is sent, and where

  • Text you choose to hear is sent to the voice service you pick, using your account, to turn it into speech: Fish Audio or ElevenLabs. Their privacy policies apply to that text. If you use your phone's built-in voices, nothing leaves your phone.
  • Webpage links you share are opened directly from your phone to get the article text.
  • Translation and text recognition use Google ML Kit and happen on your phone. Language packs are downloaded from Google the first time. ML Kit may send Google basic usage and diagnostic information. See Google's ML Kit terms.
  • Reports. If you use "Report a problem with audio", the reason, your note, the voice name and the bit of text being read are sent to Lotus Shield Studio (delivered through Discord or email), only when you press Send.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking by us.
  • We don't sell or share your information.
  • We don't read your photo library. You pick a picture each time.

AI voices

Voices from Fish Audio and ElevenLabs are AI-generated. Only use voices you own or have permission to use, and don't use Lotus Aloud to impersonate or mislead people.

Children

Lotus Aloud isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.
